# TMLR Policy Extracts (Captured 2025-11-04)

## Submission Instructions
> "We are using the reviewing and publication system of OpenReview for openness and transparency: https://openreview.net/group?id=TMLR"  
> "Before submitting to TMLR, please carefully read the Submission guidelines and editorial policies page which explains TMLR’s scope, criteria, and review process, the Author Guidelines, and also the Code of Conduct and Ethical Guidelines."  
> "Any changes to the stylefile or template that alters the formatting, font, or layout of the manuscript may result in rejection without review."

## Acceptance Criteria
> "Acceptance of a submission to TMLR should be based on positive answers to the following two questions."  
> "Are the claims made in the submission supported by accurate, convincing and clear evidence?"  
> "Would some individuals in TMLR's audience be interested in the findings of this paper?"

## Author Guidelines
> "TMLR uses a double blind review process and submissions must be anonymized."  
> "Authors may submit up to 100MB of supplementary material, such data, source code or illustrative videos; all supplementary materials must be in PDF or ZIP format."  
> "All TMLR submissions, from the time of submission to final publication, are licensed under CC BY 4.0."

## Editorial Policies
> "TMLR’s objective is to publish original papers that contribute to the understanding of the computational and mathematical principles that enable intelligence through learning, be it in brains or in machines."  
> "We do not accept submissions that are expanded versions of conference papers."  
> "TMLR uses an open-reviewing, double-blind process."  
> "The review process can be summarized in the following four phases: Submission, Reviewing, Rebuttal and discussion, Decision."
